# Bridging the neuroscience and physics of time

**Authors:** Dean Buonomano, Carlo Rovelli

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01976 - arXiv abstract, full text not retrieved

## Abstract

As a neuroscientist and a theoretical physicist, both working on time, we have
decided to open a direct dialogue to examine if the apparent discrepancies
regarding the nature of time can be composed.

## Why this matters here

Cited for one distinction and no more. This work has a sentence in Section 24.2.1
saying that nothing in it requires a body to advance along a coordinate, and that
the sense of being carried along by time is outside what is computed. That
sentence needs a reference for the claim that the sense of passage is a fact about
observers rather than about the metric, and this dialogue is the compact statement
of it from both sides at once.

It is CONTEXT and not support. Nothing in this work turns on how the sense of
passage arises, and the reference is here to mark the boundary of the claim rather
than to argue for a position about experience.
