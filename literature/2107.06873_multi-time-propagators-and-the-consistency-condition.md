# The multi-time propagators and the consistency condition

**Authors:** Siwaporn Sungted, Sikarin Yoo-Kong

**Journal:** Theoretical and Mathematical Physics, 210(2): 198-215 (2022)

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.06873

## Abstract

The time evolution of a wave function with N time variables through the Feynman picture of quantum mechanics is derived. However, these evolutions will be compatible if and only if the N Lagrangians satisfy a certain relation called the consistency condition or integrability condition which could be expressed in terms of the Wilson line. This consistency condition violates if there presents the interaction. As a consequence of this consistency condition, the evolution of the wave function gives rise to a key feature called the "path-independent" property on the space of time variables. This would suggest that one must consider all possible paths not only on the space of dependent variables but also on the space of independent variables. In the view of the geometry, this consistency condition can be considered as a zero curvature condition and the multi-time evolution can be treated as a compatible parallel transport on flat space of time variables.

## Why this matters here

This paper gives the geometric criterion that decides whether an orientation among several times can matter, and it is the most useful single statement in this batch for our question. The N time evolutions are built as Feynman propagators, one per time variable, and their mutual compatibility is expressed as a Wilson line condition on the space of times, equivalent to a zero curvature condition for the connection whose components are the N Lagrangians. When that curvature vanishes, transport in the space of times is path independent: it does not matter along which route in the multi-time space you move from the initial to the final time configuration, so an orientation among the times carries no physical information and the reduction to one time is unambiguous. When the curvature does not vanish, and the paper notes this is what happens once interaction is present, transport becomes path dependent and the route through the multi-time region leaves a measurable holonomy on the wave function. That is a direct criterion for our project: whether the orientation of the energy vector in the (3,1) region can shift the event on the single returning time axis is the question of whether the connection on the space of the three times has nonzero curvature. It also tells us where to look for the effect, in the interaction terms, since the free case is flat and therefore memoryless. The Wilson line formulation is a ready made computational object for the round trip, since the returning particle's phase would be the holonomy around the closed path in time space.
