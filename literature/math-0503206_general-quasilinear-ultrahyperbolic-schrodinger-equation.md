# The General Quasilinear Ultrahyperbolic Schrodinger Equation

**Authors:** C. E. Kenig, G. Ponce, C. Rolvung, L. Vega

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0503206 - arXiv abstract page only; full text not retrieved

## Abstract

In this work we establish a local existence theory for the initial value problem associated to the general quasi-linear ultrahyperbolic Schrodinger equation.
