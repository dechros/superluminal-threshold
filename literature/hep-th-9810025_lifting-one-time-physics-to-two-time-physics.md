# Hidden Symmetries, AdS_D x S^n, and the lifting of one-time-physics to two-time-physics

**Authors:** Itzhak Bars

**Journal:** Phys. Rev. D 59, 045019 (1999)

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9810025

## Abstract

The massive non-relativistic free particle in d-1 space dimensions has an action with a surprizing non-linearly realized SO(d,2) symmetry. This is the simplest example of a host of diverse one-time-physics systems with hidden SO(d,2) symmetric actions. By the addition of gauge degrees of freedom, they can all be lifted to the same SO(d,2) covariant unified theory that includes an extra spacelike and an extra timelike dimension. The resulting action in d+2 dimensions has manifest SO(d,2) Lorentz symmetry and a gauge symmetry Sp(2,R) and it defines two-time-physics. Conversely, the two-time action can be gauge fixed to diverse one-time physical systems. In this paper three new gauge fixed forms that correspond to the non-relativistic particle, the massive relativistic particle, and the particle in AdS_(d-n) x S^n spacetime will be discussed. The last case is discussed at the first quantized and field theory levels as well. For the last case the popularly known symmetry is SO(d-n-1,2) x SO(n+1), but yet we show that it is symmetric under the larger SO(d,2). In the field theory version the action is symmetric under the full SO(d,2) provided it is improved with a quantized mass term that arises as an anomaly from operator ordering ambiguities. The anomalous cosmological term vanishes for AdS_2 x S^0 and AdS_n x S^n (i.e. d=2n). The strikingly larger symmetry could be significant in the context of the proposed AdS/CFT duality.

## Why this matters here

This is the paper that makes the reduction of extra time explicit as a gauge choice rather than as a compactification. The d+2 worldline action carries three first class constraints X.X = 0, X.P = 0 and P.P = 0, which generate the local Sp(2,R) that acts on the doublet (X,P); fixing that gauge removes exactly one timelike and one spacelike direction and leaves an ordinary (d-1)+1 system. The decisive point for our problem is that the gauge fixing is not unique: the same 2T action reduces to the free non-relativistic particle, to the massive relativistic particle, or to a particle in AdS_(d-n) x S^n, depending on which combination of the two times is selected as the surviving evolution parameter. So the surviving single time is a choice of direction inside the higher time structure, and different choices give genuinely different dynamics and different notions of "when" on the surviving axis, while the whole family shares one SO(d,2) symmetry. The systems are not distinguished by any new degree of freedom, only by the orientation of the gauge slice, which is the exact structure we need for asking what an orientation among three times means after reduction to one. The paper also shows the reduced systems retain a hidden, non-linearly realized SO(d,2), so the memory of the extra time is not destroyed by the reduction; it survives as a symmetry acting non-linearly on the single-time variables. That non-linear action is the mechanism by which an orientation in the parent space can appear downstairs as a shift or a reparametrization of the one remaining time.
