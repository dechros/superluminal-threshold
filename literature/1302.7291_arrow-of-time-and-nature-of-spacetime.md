# The arrow of time and the nature of spacetime

**Authors:** George F. R. Ellis

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.7291 - arXiv abstract, full text not retrieved

## Abstract

This paper extends the work of a previous paper [arXiv:1208.2611] on the flow of
time, to consider the origin of the arrow of time. It proposes that a `past
condition' cascades down from cosmological to micro scales, being realized in many
microstructures and setting the arrow of time at the quantum level by top-down
causation. This physics arrow of time then propagates up, through underlying
emergence of higher level structures, to geology, astronomy, engineering, and
biology. The appropriate space-time picture to view all this is an emergent block
universe (`EBU'), that recognizes the way the present is different from both the
past and the future. This essential difference is the ultimate reason the arrow of
time has to be the way it is.

## Why this matters here

The strongest published position against the reading Section 8.4 leaves open, and
it should be carried as such rather than omitted. Section 8.4 finds that the
metric does not distinguish the two causal components and concludes that whatever
does lies outside the geometry. Ellis agrees that it lies outside the local
equations and then supplies it from a global boundary condition, cascading down by
top-down causation, in a spacetime that grows rather than simply existing.

That is a direct rival to this work at the point where it matters. If the
direction is fixed globally, the sign of the crossing is fixed too, and the even
division of the admissible crossings found in Section 2.1 would be broken from
outside rather than left open. The division would still be a fact about the
transformations; it would just no longer be a freedom. This work does not adopt a
past condition and does not argue against one, so the honest statement is that
Section 2.1's freedom is conditional on there being no such global selector, and
that condition is now named.

Status against the article: CHALLENGE to the openness of the selection, and the
cleanest statement of what would close it.
