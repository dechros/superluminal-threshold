# Quantum measurements of time

**Authors:** Lorenzo Maccone, Krzysztof Sacha

**Journal:** Phys. Rev. Lett. 124, 110402 (2020)

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.12869 - arXiv abstract page only; full text not retrieved

## Abstract

We propose a time-of-arrival operator in quantum mechanics by conditioning on a quantum clock. This allows us to bypass some of the problems of previous proposals, and to obtain a Hermitian time of arrival operator whose probability distribution arises from the Born rule and which has a clear physical interpretation. The same procedure can be employed to measure the "time at which some event happens" for arbitrary events (and not just specifically for the arrival time of a particle).

## Why this matters here

Maccone and Sacha give a time of arrival operator built by conditioning on an explicit quantum clock degree of freedom, which is the structural answer to the difficulty that time is a parameter and not an observable in standard quantum mechanics. By enlarging the system to include a clock and conditioning on its readings, they obtain a Hermitian time of arrival operator whose probability distribution follows from the Born rule, bypassing the self adjointness obstruction that blocks the older Aharonov-Bohm and Kijowski constructions, and the same construction gives the time at which an arbitrary event happens, not just an arrival. This is directly usable in our setting. Our particle has no proper time inside the signature-changed region, so any statement about when it returns must be referred to an external degree of freedom, and this paper says what that degree of freedom has to be and how to condition on it. It also reframes what we are computing: the Wigner phase derivative is a property of the amplitude, whereas the conditional construction produces a distribution over readings of a physical clock, and only the second is an observable. If our article wants to claim a return moment rather than a phase, this is the framework in which the claim has to be phrased, and the difference between the two would itself be a result worth reporting.
